  <section class="hb-section">
    <h2>Research Interests</h2>
    <p>
      My research lies at the intersection of optimization under uncertainty and data-driven decision making, with a focus on mixed-integer programming, decomposition and cutting-plane methods, and AI-based algorithm design for large-scale optimization.
    </p>
    <ul>
      <li>Stochastic and distributionally robust optimization</li>
      <li>Mixed-integer programming and decomposition algorithms</li>
      <li>Learning-augmented optimization methods</li>
      <li>Applications in power systems and energy operations</li>
    </ul>
  </section>

  <section class="hb-section">
    <h2>Current Focus</h2>
    <ul>
      <li>Globally convergent cutting-plane algorithms for multistage stochastic mixed-integer programs</li>
      <li>Efficient approximation methods for chance-constrained optimization with ambiguity</li>
      <li>AI-assisted algorithm selection and acceleration in branch-and-cut frameworks</li>
    </ul>
  </section>

  <section class="hb-section">
    <h2>Research Style</h2>
    <p>
      I enjoy research that combines rigorous theory, scalable algorithm design, and practical impact. I am especially interested in projects where methodological advances can be validated through convincing computational experiments and real-world motivated applications.
    </p>
