<div class="profile-page">

I am currently a Research Fellow in the [Department of Information Systems, Business Statistics and Operations Management](https://isom.hkust.edu.hk) (ISOM) at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology, working with [Prof. Guodong Lyu](https://isom.hkust.edu.hk/faculty-and-staff/directory/imlyu).

My research focuses on optimization under uncertainty, stochastic mixed-integer programming, and AI-based algorithm design, with applications in power systems and operations management.

I received my Ph.D. in Data Science from [The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen](https://sds.cuhk.edu.cn), advised by [Prof. Haoxiang Yang](https://sites.google.com/view/haoxiangyang), and my B.S. in Statistics from [Southern University of Science and Technology](https://math.sustech.edu.cn/?lang=en).
